AgentCut distributes unmodified FFmpeg and ffprobe executables from these
static-build providers:

- macOS arm64: https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de/
- Windows x64: https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/

Those builds are licensed under GNU GPL version 3. The complete GPLv3 license
text is staged alongside the binaries as FFMPEG-GPLv3.txt by
scripts/stage-ffmpeg.mjs. FFmpeg source and licensing information:
https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html
